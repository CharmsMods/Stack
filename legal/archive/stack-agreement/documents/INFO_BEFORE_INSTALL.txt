BEFORE INSTALLING STACK

Stack does not require an account, activation, payment, or permission to use.

After you accept the EULA, Stack checks the official GitHub Releases API for
updates when the app starts. You can disable "Check automatically when Stack
starts" in Settings > Updates. Stack does not include intentional product
analytics or a Stack-operated telemetry service.

The installer places the EULA, Privacy Notice, Stack Source License,
Third-Party Notices, and exact dependency license texts under:

  Stack\App\Legal

You can also open these documents from Settings > Legal inside Stack.

The unchanged official installer may be shared with other people free of
charge. It may not be sold, modified, repackaged, rebranded, or presented as
someone else's product.
