# 04 — Color-Grading Operations

## Purpose

Color grading changes color relationships rather than only scalar tone. Some controls are simple pointwise math; others are convenience systems built from color conversion, tonal masks, curves, matrices, gamut handling, and blending. Their names are especially ambiguous across applications.

The decisive question is not merely “what math?” but **what coordinates does the math act on?** Saturation in linear RGB, encoded RGB, HSV, Lab, and OKLCH produces different colors. Temperature may mean crude red/blue offsets, camera-native white-balance gains, or a chromatic-adaptation transform between measured white points. A color-space conversion is only defined when primaries, transfer functions, white points, adaptation, range, and alpha handling are known.

Stack currently has Saturation, Warmth, 3-Way Grade, LUT, RAW WB/camera matrices, and a View Transform, but generic wires are color-state-free. The table therefore distinguishes implemented numeric behavior from a trustworthy color-managed contract.

## Primary balance, white balance, and global chroma

| Stable ID | Operation | Canonical formula / algorithm | Scope | Required domain / state | What it does / common uses | Composition cautions | Current Stack status based on audit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `COLOR.RGB_BALANCE` | RGB balance | Chosen per-channel gain and/or offset: `rgb'=rgb⊙g+o` | Pointwise RGB | Named encoding, gain/offset method, alpha policy | Bias overall color toward/away from channels | Gain and offset are not interchangeable; encoded and linear results differ; not automatically white balance | **Composable** through Split/DataMath/Combine; no direct general node |
| `COLOR.WHEELS` | Color wheels | `out = combine(in, adjust(in,c), w(L))`, where wheel color `c` maps to a declared RGB/opponent adjustment | Pointwise with tone-derived weights | Named color model, tone metric, zone weights and blend method | Intuitive shadow/mid/high color adjustment | A wheel is a UI over several algorithms, not a primitive; neutral point, luminance preservation, overlap and clamp must be explicit | **Implemented as part of 3-Way Color Grade**, Stable; hard-coded Rec.709 tonal weights and mixed additive/multiplicative chroma |
| `COLOR.TEMPERATURE` | Temperature | A named method: camera RGB gains from neutral/illuminant, or chromatic adaptation from source white to target white | Pointwise once parameters are known | Source/target white chromaticities or camera spectral/profile data | Warm/cool white-balance movement | Kelvin alone is insufficient off the Planckian/daylight locus; a red/blue slider is only a stylized warmth control | **Absent** accurately; Warmth is NeedsFix and only does `R+=.1a`, `B-=.1a` |
| `COLOR.TINT` | Tint | Orthogonal white-point shift, commonly green↔magenta in a specified chromaticity/uv system, then adaptation/gains | Pointwise once parameters are known | White-point/tint coordinate system and source profile | Correct green/magenta illuminant cast | There is no universal “tint unit”; coupling with temperature and camera sensitivities matters | **Absent** |
| `COLOR.WB_GAINS` | White-balance gains | Camera/RGB diagonal transform `rgb'=diag(g_R,g_G,g_B)rgb`, gains derived from selected neutral | Pointwise | Preferably linear camera-native RGB plus neutral metadata/user sample | Neutralize capture illuminant; creative WB offset | In display RGB it is an approximation; gain normalization changes exposure/headroom; metadata is a choice, not ground truth | **Implemented RAW-specifically** before camera transform; no general metadata-aware WB node |
| `COLOR.CAT` | Chromatic adaptation | Von Kries/CAT form `XYZ_d = M⁻¹ diag((M W_d)/(M W_s)) M XYZ_s` using a named CAT | Pointwise matrix transform | XYZ plus source/destination white points and CAT (Bradford, CAT16, etc.) | Convert appearance between adopted whites | Must not be applied twice; CAT choice/viewing assumptions matter; adaptation is not merely temperature | **Absent** generically |
| `COLOR.SATURATION` | Saturation | One RGB method: `gray=w·rgb`; `out=gray+s(rgb-gray)` | Pointwise | Chosen space, achromatic axis/luma weights and range | Move colors away from neutral | RGB method may change luminance/hue and can leave gamut; “saturation” differs from chroma/colorfulness | **Implemented, NeedsFix**: Rec.709-weighted gray and `mix(gray,rgb,1+amount)` on untyped data |
| `COLOR.DESATURATION` | Desaturation | Same selected saturation method with `0≤s<1`; `s=0` reaches its defined neutral axis | Pointwise | Same as Saturation | Reduce color, create grayscale at zero | The grayscale endpoint depends on method; applying in encoded RGB is a look, not physical luminance | **Implemented through negative Saturation amount**, with same caveats |
| `COLOR.VIBRANCE` | Vibrance | Heuristic/adaptive saturation, e.g. `s_eff=f(existing_chroma,hue,skin_confidence,user_amount)` | Pointwise or model-assisted | Named color model, protection/roll-off rules | Increase muted colors more than already strong colors | No canonical formula; “skin protection” is optional, not inherent; must document heuristic | **Absent** |

## Hue, lightness, and color-model coordinates

| Stable ID | Operation | Canonical formula / algorithm | Scope | Required domain / state | What it does / common uses | Composition cautions | Current Stack status based on audit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `COLOR.HUE_ROTATE` | Hue-axis rotation | Convert to a declared opponent/vector space; rotate chromatic plane by angle `θ`; convert back | Pointwise | Named space/axis/white point; radians/degrees | Continuous global hue rotation | Rotation in YIQ, OKLab, IPT, HSV, etc. is not equivalent; gamut mapping may be required | **Absent** |
| `COLOR.HUE_SHIFT` | Cylindrical hue shift | In HSV/HSL/LCH/OKLCH-like coordinates: `h'=(h+Δh) mod 360°` | Pointwise | Named cylindrical space; powerless-hue policy | Directly shift hue coordinate | Near neutral, hue may be undefined/powerless; cylindrical wrap/interpolation direction matters | **Absent**; retain separately from vector rotation only if both methods are explicit |
| `COLOR.LIGHTNESS` | Perceptual lightness adjustment | Convert to Lab/OKLab/CAM space; modify `L` with a declared offset/scale/curve; convert back | Pointwise | Perceptual space, white point/viewing conditions and gamut policy | Change perceived light/dark coordinate | Not brightness, luma, luminance, HSV value, or HSL lightness unless explicitly selected | **Absent** |
| `COLOR.CHROMA` | Chroma adjustment | In LCh/OKLCH: `C=√(a²+b²)`; set `C'=f(C)` while retaining `h=atan2(b,a)` | Pointwise | Named opponent space/white point | Adjust distance from neutral at a given lightness | Constant hue lines curve perceptually and destination gamut varies with L/h; neutral hue is undefined | **Absent** |
| `COLOR.COLORFULNESS` | Colorfulness adjustment | Modify a named appearance-model colorfulness correlate such as CAM16 `M` | Pointwise with viewing parameters | Color appearance model, adapting luminance, surround, white | Viewing-condition-relative strength of color appearance | Not a simple synonym for saturation; meaningless without viewing conditions | **Absent** |
| `COLOR.MODEL_CONVERT` | RGB/HSV/HSL/HWB/Lab/OKLab family conversion | Apply the exact forward/inverse equations of the named model | Pointwise coordinate transform | Exact model version, RGB basis/transfer, white point and ranges | Expose hue/chroma/lightness controls or analysis coordinates | HSV/HSL are geometric rearrangements of encoded RGB, not perceptually uniform color spaces; round trips have powerless-hue/precision issues | **Absent** as a generic transform; recommended primitive family |
| `COLOR.DELTA_E` | Perceptual color difference | Named metric, e.g. `ΔE_OK=‖OKLab₁-OKLab₂‖` or CIEDE2000 | Pointwise two-color scalar output | Both colors in required reference space; metric/version | Color matching, keys, tolerances, validation | “Distance” without a space/metric is undefined; CIEDE2000 and Euclidean OKLab are not interchangeable | **Absent** generically; target-color nodes use local RGB-distance heuristics |

## Selective and curve-driven grading

| Stable ID | Operation | Canonical formula / algorithm | Scope | Required domain / state | What it does / common uses | Composition cautions | Current Stack status based on audit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `COLOR.SELECTIVE` | Selective color | Build soft weight `m=color_range(rgb)`; blend `out=lerp(in,grade(in),m)` | Pointwise, sometimes spatially refined | Named selection space, center/range/falloff, transform and gamut policy | Adjust chosen color families | Mask and grade are separate primitives; hue wrap, neutral colors, and luminance dependence matter | **Partially composable** using Sampled Range Mask + grade; no dedicated selective-color node |
| `COLOR.HUE_VS_HUE` | Hue versus hue | Extract `h`; curve supplies `Δh=f(h)`; apply wrapped hue shift | Pointwise curve-driven | Named hue space, curve interpolation and neutral policy | Remap selected hues | Curve must wrap continuously at 0/360; saturation/lightness limits often needed near neutral | **Absent** |
| `COLOR.HUE_VS_SAT` | Hue versus saturation | `s'=s·g(h)` or `s'=f(h,s)` according to declared model | Pointwise curve-driven | Named hue/saturation model and curve | Saturate/desaturate chosen hue sectors | Multiplicative vs additive semantics differ; out-of-gamut handling required | **Absent** |
| `COLOR.HUE_VS_LUMA` | Hue versus luminance/lightness | Extract hue; apply a named tone/lightness adjustment weighted by `f(h)` | Pointwise curve-driven | Hue space plus selected luma/luminance/lightness definition | Brighten/darken selected hues | Original label “luminance” must not silently mean brightness/lightness; hue can shift after tone change | **Absent** |
| `COLOR.LUMA_VS_SAT` | Luma versus saturation | `s'=s·g(L)` using a selected `L` metric | Pointwise curve-driven | Named luma/luminance/lightness and saturation model | Control color strength by tonal region | Rec.709 coefficients on untyped values are not guaranteed luma/luminance; avoid shadow noise amplification | **Absent** as generic; tonal saturation exists inside some effects |
| `COLOR.SAT_VS_SAT` | Saturation versus saturation | `s'=f(s)` | Pointwise curve-driven | Named saturation coordinate/range | Roll off intense colors or lift muted colors | Often part of vibrance/gamut compression; monotonicity and gamut policy matter | **Absent** |
| `COLOR.REPLACE` | Color replacement | Soft color-distance/key mask plus target mapping; `out=lerp(in,replacement(in,target),m)` | Pointwise plus optional spatial refinement | Selection metric, target method, tone-preservation rule | Replace a color family while retaining shading | Flat replacement, hue rotation, and reference-statistic matching are distinct; avoid edge contamination | **Partial / NeedsFix**: Background Remover/Patcher has color-distance matte behavior but is not a complete replacement node |
| `COLOR.SIX_VECTOR` | Six-vector / hue-sector grade | Build overlapping weights for R,Y,G,C,B,M sectors; apply hue/sat/value changes; normalize overlaps | Pointwise compound | Hue model, sector centers/widths, overlap normalization | Familiar six-primary color correction | Hard sectors band; soft sectors must wrap and sum predictably; output gamut handling required | **Absent** |

| Stable ID | Operation | Canonical formula / algorithm | Scope | Required domain / state | What it does / common uses | Composition cautions | Current Stack status based on audit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `COLOR.SPLIT_TONE` | Split toning | Shadow/highlight weights from tone metric; blend separate chroma adjustments, often with balance pivot | Pointwise compound | Tone metric/weights, color adjustment model and blend | Tint shadows and highlights separately | Zone overlap, luminance preservation, balance, and working space define the look | **Absent** as dedicated node; composable from masks + grades |
| `COLOR.BALANCE` | Color balance | Bias shadows, mids, highlights through named zone weights and RGB/opponent adjustments | Pointwise compound | Same contract as color wheels | Conventional global tonal color correction | Broad UI category; often synonymous with three-way grading, so avoid duplicate implementations | **Implemented effectively as 3-Way Color Grade** |
| `COLOR.THREE_WAY` | Three-way grade | Three tonal weights `w_s,w_m,w_h` plus three color adjustments; combine with explicit overlap/normalization | Pointwise compound | Tone metric, wheel math, strength/blend and gamut policy | Independent shadow/mid/high grade | Not one standard formula; Stack currently uses additive shadows/mids and multiplicative highlights, which must be documented | **Implemented, Stable** as 3-Way Color Grade |
| `COLOR.DENSITY` | Color density | A selected film-inspired formula coupling channel darkening with chroma/richness | Pointwise compound | Named density model and working encoding | Darken a color while increasing perceived richness | Highly nonstandard; do not ship a formula-free “Density” primitive; can alter exposure and gamut sharply | **Absent** |
| `COLOR.PRESERVE_LUMA` | Luminance/luma preservation constraint | After color change, correct selected metric, e.g. scale by `Y_in/max(Y_out,ε)` or solve in a space with fixed lightness | Pointwise constraint | Named metric, zero policy and gamut strategy | Hold brightness measure while changing color | Ratio correction can explode near black and cause out-of-gamut values; “luma” and luminance differ | **Node-specific at best**; no reusable constraint node |

| Stable ID | Operation | Canonical formula / algorithm | Scope | Required domain / state | What it does / common uses | Composition cautions | Current Stack status based on audit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `COLOR.GAMUT_TEST` | In-gamut test | Convert to destination linear RGB; test all components against destination bounds with tolerance | Pointwise Boolean/mask | Fully defined destination gamut/encoding and tolerance | Build out-of-gamut warnings/masks | Negative RGB can be valid intermediate math but is outside a simple destination RGB cube; profile/rendering intent may define a more complex condition | **Absent** |
| `COLOR.GAMUT_COMPRESS` | Gamut compression | Nonlinear map pulling colors toward an achromatic axis or gamut boundary while aiming to preserve hue/lightness | Pointwise color-managed | Source/destination gamut, working perceptual space, boundary and compression curve | Fit extreme colors smoothly into target gamut | No universal formula; threshold/limit behavior and hue curvature must be specified; can be invertible only over part of range | **Implemented heuristically inside View Transform**; no general facility |
| `COLOR.GAMUT_CLIP` | Gamut clipping | Simplest RGB-cube form: `rgb'=clamp(rgb,0,1)` after conversion to destination RGB | Pointwise | Destination RGB basis/range | Hard-limit out-of-gamut channels | This is only one clipping method and can distort hue/lightness; do not call every channel clamp gamut mapping | **Widespread node-local final clamps**; no semantic destination-gamut node |
| `COLOR.LUT` | 1D / 3D LUT application | Normalize by declared domain; sample 1D channels or 3D cube with selected interpolation | Pointwise lookup | Domain min/max, table size, shaper, input/output spaces, interpolation, clamp/extrapolation | Technical transforms and creative looks | LUT files may not declare color spaces; 3D trilinear/tetrahedral and 1D interpolation differ; clamp loses HDR/out-of-domain data | **Implemented**: `.cube` domain and 1D/3D tables honored; configurable sRGB/gamma transfer; primaries/white/input/output intent undeclared |
| `COLOR.SPACE_CONVERT` | Color-space conversion | Named pipeline: decode source transfer → source RGB→XYZ matrix → chromatic adaptation if needed → XYZ→destination RGB → encode destination transfer | Pointwise; ICC/LUT variants possible | Complete source/destination definitions, rendering intent, range and alpha | Preserve color appearance while changing representation | Matrix alone is insufficient; scene/display conversions may require tone/gamut mapping beyond colorimetric conversion | **No general node**; RAW camera matrices and LUT transfer modes are isolated pieces |
| `COLOR.DISPLAY_TRANSFORM` | Display transform | Scene space → tone scale → gamut mapping → display linear RGB → output OETF/EOTF code values | Pointwise plus display metadata | Scene space, display primaries, peak/black, transfer and viewing conditions | Render scene-referred data for a target display | Distinguish from a look and from simple color conversion; target-specific and generally lossy | **View Transform is Stable but incomplete**: tone/gamut mapping and clamp exist; final sRGB OETF/display contract is missing |

## Ambiguity rules for Stack

- **Warmth is not temperature.** Stack’s current Warmth node is a red/blue code-value offset. Keep it as a stylized adjustment or replace it with a clearly selected method.
- **Saturation, chroma, and colorfulness are not synonyms.** Saturation is relative, chroma is distance from neutral in a specified space, and colorfulness depends on viewing conditions in advanced appearance models.
- **Hue rotation and hue-coordinate shift** may remain separate only if the space/algorithm differs visibly; otherwise they are aliases.
- **Color wheels, color balance, and three-way grading** should share one compound definition with UI presets, not become three drifting implementations.
- **Preserve hue/luma** are constraints applied to another transform. They should be options or reusable helper compounds rather than mysterious independent effects.
- **Color-space conversion, display transform, and look transform** occupy different pipeline roles and must never be represented by one vague “Color Transform” mode without source/destination/intent fields.

## Recommended Stack treatment

- Implement first-class color-state metadata and general Color Space Convert before multiplying the number of grading nodes.
- Add reusable primitives for `Mat3`, named transfer encode/decode, model conversion, hue/chroma/lightness extraction, color distance, soft range masks, and gamut test/compression.
- Rebuild Saturation, Warmth/Temperature, 3-Way, selective curves, split tone, and six-vector as inspectable compounds over those primitives.
- Keep LUT as a specialized efficient node, but require or sidecar-store intended input/output spaces and show an explicit warning when unknown.
- Complete View Transform with a target display/OETF stage and prevent display-ready results from becoming indistinguishable from working scene-linear images.
